# My Life in 15 B.C.
**February, 2008.** I was twenty-one, three years out of high school, and killing time behind the counter of an adult video store while a blizzard buried the city of Somerville, Massachusetts. No college degree, no plan, no prayer life -- just a string of dead-end jobs and a growing concern that this was as good as my life was ever going to get.

It must have been around nine o'clock in the evening, and a blizzard was raging on outside. A few bottles of liquor sat on the floor by my feet. The store wasn't very busy and it never really was. But it was busier than you'd expect considering the storm. Maybe it had something to do with the kind of videos we rented: pornography. I was watching TV, leaned back in my chair, feet on the counter. I heard the door open behind me and felt the cold wind rush in. I didn't acknowledge the customer. That's not something you usually do in this kind of store. The customer walked past the desk and down into the store. I recognized his jacket and his haircut: it was my English teacher from the community college. It's not the first time someone I knew came into this store without knowing I worked there. A few minutes later he was on his way back to the counter. I haven't moved an inch, and my eyes are trained on the TV mounted up on a wall. He must have recognized me because as he approached, he quietly placed his video on a shelf near the counter and walked briskly out the door. Curious, I got up to see what he had left behind: *Tranny Shop*. I can't say I was surprised. 

Some time after that awkward moment, two women I knew walked in: Katie, and her best friend, the other Katie. On that night, none of us would have ever guessed that one Katie would wind up being my wife and mother of my children, and the other would become Godmother to my second child.
