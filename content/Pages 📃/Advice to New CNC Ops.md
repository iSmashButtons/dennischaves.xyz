In the grand scheme of things, I haven't been working as a machinist *for that long*. But I think I have learned a few things over the last decade or so that might help a newcomer. Having worked on the shop floor for many years, and having worked as a supervisor for one year, I think I have a thing or two to say that might help you be successful in your own role.
