Machinist by day, maker and computer nerd by night. I leave the plant at 3 PM but the workshop in my head never closes! I enjoy using my 3D printer to come up with new games, toys, and gizmos for my kids, as well as for home improvement and general tinkering. Every chore in the house, or repair to be made, is a challenge to find the most efficient and practical solution.
